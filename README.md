## What is Islamic wall art?

A bare wall can make a home feel unfinished. For many Muslim families, the answer is art that reflects faith, calm and belonging. [Islamic wall art](https://wafanoor.com/product-categories/islamic-glass-wall-art) brings Arabic calligraphy, short duas and verses into the home as a quiet daily reminder, while also working as a design element in the room.

Good Islamic wall art does two jobs at once. It completes a space visually, and it gives the people who live there something meaningful to look at. It also works for guests who cannot read Arabic, because the flowing lines of calligraphy are beautiful in their own right.

## Why choose glass?

Glass pieces have become one of the most popular forms of Islamic wall art, and for good reason.

- **Depth and shine:** glass catches light and looks different in the morning than it does at sunset.
- **Modern look:** a clean glass surface suits contemporary, minimalist and classic interiors alike.
- **Easy care:** a soft dry cloth is usually enough to keep the surface looking fresh.
- **Lasting color:** quality prints stay sharp over time when hung away from harsh direct sun.

## How to choose the right piece

### 1. Start with the words

The text matters more than the frame. Some popular choices and where they are often placed:

| Phrase | Common placement |
| --- | --- |
| Ayat al-Kursi (Quran 2:255) | Living room, entryway |
| Bismillah | Kitchen, dining area |
| Shahada | Living room, hallway |
| Subhanallah / Alhamdulillah | Bedroom, reading corner |
| Names of Allah | Study area, prayer corner |

Always check that the calligraphy is accurate and spelled correctly. The words deserve respect, and mistakes are hard to unsee.

### 2. Match your colors

- **Modern homes:** black, white and gold look sharp and clean.
- **Warm, earthy homes:** beige, brown and soft green blend in naturally.
- **Calm, cool rooms:** blue and turquoise tones add freshness.
- **Busy rooms:** choose one large piece rather than several small ones.

### 3. Pick the right size

A piece that is too small looks lost, and one that is too large can overwhelm the room. A good rule is to cover about two thirds of the wall width above a sofa, bed or console. Before buying, cut paper to size and tape it to the wall to see how it looks.

### 4. Think about light

Glass shines in rooms with natural light, and warm lamps bring out the details in the evening. Avoid hanging it where direct sun hits all day.

## Where to hang it

1. **Living room:** above the sofa or console, at eye level.
2. **Entryway:** a short welcoming phrase sets a calm tone as guests arrive.
3. **Bedroom:** soft, neutral pieces above the bed or on a quiet wall.
4. **Prayer corner:** at a comfortable height in front of you, away from clutter.
5. **Dining area:** a gratitude-themed piece fits naturally here.

Keep pieces with sacred text in clean, respectful places and away from areas such as bathrooms.

## Care tips

- Wipe with a soft, dry microfiber cloth.
- Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive cleaners.
- Use hanging hardware suited to your wall type and the weight of the piece.
- Keep the surface away from moisture and heat sources.

## FAQ

**What is the best Islamic wall art for a living room?**
Large calligraphy pieces such as Ayat al-Kursi or the Shahada work well above a sofa. Choose a color that fits your existing palette.

**Is glass wall art good for modern homes?**
Yes. Glass has a clean, polished look that suits modern, minimalist and classic interiors.

**Can I give Islamic wall art as a gift?**
Absolutely. It is a popular choice for housewarmings, weddings, Ramadan and Eid, because it stays in the home for years.

**How do I know the calligraphy is accurate?**
Buy from a seller that shows clear photos and uses verified Arabic text, and check the phrase against a trusted source before hanging it.
